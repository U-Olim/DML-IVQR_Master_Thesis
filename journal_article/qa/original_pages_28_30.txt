D.4    Numerics, failures, and reproduction workflow
Scores use an uncentered second moment with denominator 𝑛 and no finite-sample correction. Matrix
inversions are direct, without generalized inverses or added regularization. Errors and non-finite statistics
are stored with design, replication, estimator, candidate, stage, and message, and yield missing statistics.
Incomplete profiles receive missing aggregate measures. The main run has zero recorded numerical
failures. The grid diagnostic’s single Stage-1 error is recorded in Appendix A; Stage 2 has no new
evaluation failures.
    Point estimates select the first exact minimizer on the ordered 41-point grid. Acceptance is 𝑊𝑛 (𝑎) ≤
5.991464547 . . ., with an asymptotic two-degree-of-freedom chi-square cutoff. Coverage and each power
alternative are evaluated directly, separately from stored grid profiles. Domain diagnostics retain interiors
and add tails on the same samples. Neither numerical inversion nor a density proxy establishes the ideal
regularity conditions.
    The frozen workflow, documented in docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_NOTES.md, first runs thesis_
extension/final/run_final.R separately with –sample-size=500 and –sample-size=1000, each
with –execute-final. Calibration, grid Stage 1 and triggered Stage 2, and the coverage audit follow
before reporting. The reporting builder requires the freeze commit, all empirical inputs, deliberately
empty output directories, and absent reporting manifests; it refuses to replace existing reporting outputs.
Simulation scripts have no general overwrite safeguard, so regeneration requires preserving the canonical
archive and preparing separate output locations. Seed and environment records do not imply a universal
one-command reproduction guarantee. Machine-readable records and full numerical tables are retained
without duplicating simulation datasets in this article package.
